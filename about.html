About Kirana List

Simplifying your shop preparation.

Prepare Smarter

Kirana List Builder is a simple, visual web application designed to help you prepare your grocery shopping list before you head to the store.

Key Features

  • ✓ Visual Catalog: Detailed images for every grocery item.
  • ✓ Quick Add: Tap to add items to your list instantly.
  • ✓ Privacy First: Your list is saved on your device, not our servers.
  • ✓ Easy Sharing: Copy your list as text for WhatsApp or SMS.

Installable

This app is designed as a Mobile-First PWA. You can add it to your home screen for quick access, just like a native app.
