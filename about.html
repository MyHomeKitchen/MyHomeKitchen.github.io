About My Home Kitchen

🛒 What is My Home Kitchen?

My Home Kitchen is a smart, intuitive shopping list application designed to simplify your grocery shopping experience. Whether you're planning your weekly groceries, stocking up on household essentials, or preparing for a special occasion, this app makes it easy to organize and manage your shopping needs.

Built with modern web technologies, My Home Kitchen offers a comprehensive catalog of products across multiple categories, allowing you to quickly build your shopping list with just a few clicks. The app is completely free, works offline, and respects your privacy by storing all data locally in your browser.

✨ Key Features

📦 180+ Products

Comprehensive catalog across 29 categories including groceries, household items, and more

🌍 Multi-Language

Supports 8 languages: English, Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Kannada, Bengali

🖼️ Visual Catalog

Browse products with real images for easy identification

✏️ Custom Items

Add any item not in the catalog to personalize your list

📤 Export & Share

Share your list as an image, PDF, or text

🌙 Dark Mode

Easy on the eyes with automatic theme switching

💾 Auto-Save

Your list is automatically saved locally in your browser

📱 Mobile-First

Optimized for phones, tablets, and desktop devices

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your privacy matters. My Home Kitchen stores all data locally in your browser using localStorage. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no data collection. Everything stays on your device.

🌐 Technology Stack

Built with modern web technologies including:

  • React - For building the user interface
  • Vite - For fast development and optimized builds
  • i18next - For internationalization and multi-language support
  • Lucide React - For beautiful, consistent icons
  • HTML2Canvas - For exporting lists as images

🚀 Open Source

My Home Kitchen is an open-source project. The source code is available on GitHub, and contributions are welcome! Whether you want to report a bug, suggest a feature, or contribute code, we'd love to hear from you.
